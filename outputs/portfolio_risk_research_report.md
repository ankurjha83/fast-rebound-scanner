## Explicit answers

1. **What caused the approximately -30% drawdowns?** The evidence is most consistent with **correlated high-beta portfolio exposure during broad risk-off periods**, combined with a small number of large losing trade windows.
2. **Primary mechanism?** **Too many simultaneous, correlated, high-beta positions during broad selloffs.** Normal-period 60-day correlation/positions/beta/exposure average 0.37/1.8/0.37/17.05%; in >20% drawdowns they rise to 0.64/7.3/1.35/67.37%. The deepest episode coincides with SPY -14.49%, QQQ -16.11%, and VIX 165.63%; semiconductor/AI exposure is prominent. Individual losses contribute, but no single catastrophic trade is the primary portfolio mechanism.
3. **Does reducing position count help?** **Yes.** Five positions returns 64.07% with 0.440 Sharpe and -17.42% drawdown. Three positions reaches -13.79%, but sacrifices more return and provides almost no Sharpe improvement.
4. **Does smaller sizing help beyond lower exposure?** **No.** A 5% position size cuts drawdown to -16.18%, but Sharpe falls to 0.396; its drawdown is no better than proportional exposure scaling predicts.
5. **Does an exposure cap help?** **Yes.** The 50% cap produces 65.15%, 0.449 Sharpe, and -18.39% drawdown. It is useful, but the five-position rule is simpler and scores better.
6. **Does a beta cap help?** **Partly.** Beta 1.5 produces 73.24%, 0.427, and -21.47%; the tighter 1.0 cap suppresses Sharpe materially.
7. **Does a theme cap help?** **Yes, but not enough alone.** The 30% cap reaches 88.43%/0.471/-23.70%; the 20% cap reaches -20.87% with 0.472 Sharpe.
8. **Does the correlation rule help?** **No.** The single 60-day/0.75 rule leaves drawdown at -30.99% and lowers Sharpe to 0.359.
9. **Does QQQ risk-off management help?** **No.** It produces -32.95% drawdown and 0.359 Sharpe, both worse than the unmanaged range strategy.
10. **Does the circuit breaker help?** **No.** It reduces activity to 7.94% average exposure, return to 19.72%, and Sharpe to 0.221; the improvement is primarily inactivity.
11. **Best individual rule?** **max_positions_5** leads the predeclared drawdown/Sharpe/development score.
12. **Can up to three rules improve the strategy?** The combined row uses only independently useful categories: max_positions_5, theme_cap_30pct. It is not preferred when dominated by max_positions_5.
